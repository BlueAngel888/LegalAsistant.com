---
name: Auditing uv projects
description: How to get a reliable Python dependency audit when pip-audit does not recognize a uv lockfile.
---
For a uv-managed project, do not assume pip-audit's project-path `--locked` option understands `uv.lock`. Audit a pinned requirements export generated by uv from the lockfile, without re-resolving dependencies.

**Why:** pip-audit reported “no lockfiles found” against the project even though uv had an up-to-date lockfile. An audit of uv's pinned requirements export succeeded.

**How to apply:** Keep the uv lockfile current, export it to requirements format, and feed that pinned export to pip-audit with no dependency resolution; verify the result against the exact locked packages.
